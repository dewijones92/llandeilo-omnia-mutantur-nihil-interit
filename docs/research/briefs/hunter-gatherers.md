# Brief: hunter-gatherers in and around the Tywi valley

Given to a research agent (Claude Opus 5.5) on 2026-10-02. The work was agreed by Dewi as "early
people hunting stuff" (todo: "Early people hunting and gathering"). The task text is summarised
closely below; the output-format boilerplate is abbreviated.

## What was asked

Research hunter-gatherers in and around the Tywi valley from the first return of people after the
ice (c. 12,500 BC in calendar years?) to the start of farming (c. 4000 BC):

1. **Evidence**: inside the ten miles (Archwilio/Heneb HER finds: flint scatters, microliths, any
   Mesolithic or Late Upper Palaeolithic site near Llandeilo, the Black Mountain, the Tywi gravels);
   nearby (Waun Fignen Felen, Coygan, Paviland, Burry Holms, the Nab Head, Goldcliff); and Wales-wide.
2. **What was hunted, fished and gathered** in each environment phase, in calendar years: the Late
   Glacial, the Younger Dryas, the early Holocene birch and pine woods, the later wildwood; animal
   bones and dates from Welsh sites; the use of fire (Waun Fignen Felen).
3. **How they lived and hunted**: tools (Creswellian points, microliths, bows, with Stellmoor and
   Holmegaard as analogy), dogs, clothing, camps and shelters (Howick as analogy), seasonal movement,
   group size; burial and art in Wales, taking care over the much earlier Red Lady of Paviland.
4. **What a scene or cutscene could honestly show**, each element with a tier: documented here,
   documented nearby, Wales or Britain analogy, or imagined detail.
5. **Animals present in each phase**, with sourced date ranges and when each died out, linked to the
   deep-time note.
