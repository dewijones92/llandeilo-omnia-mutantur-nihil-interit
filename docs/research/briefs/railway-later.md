# Brief: the later railway at Llandeilo, 1889 to today

Given to a research agent (Claude Opus 5.5) on 2026-10-02. The work was agreed by Dewi as the todo
"Research the later trains (Great Western from 1889, British Rail, today's Heart of Wales line)".
The task text is summarised closely below; the output-format boilerplate is abbreviated.

## What was asked

Research the later trains and railway at Llandeilo, carrying on from
[`railway-locomotives.md`](../railway-locomotives.md) (the first trains, 1857–1888):

- the Great Western Railway takeover (c. 1889?) and the GWR era;
- the Big Four, and nationalisation as British Railways Western Region in 1948;
- the Beeching era: the closure of the Llandeilo–Carmarthen line (1963–65?) and the threats to the
  Central Wales line;
- diesel multiple units;
- the Heart of Wales line today: operator, typical rolling stock, service pattern;
- the Llandeilo station building, and "Llandeilo Junction" and the line south to Llandybïe and
  Ammanford (the Llanelly Railway's route).

For each period: which locomotive classes or units actually worked trains through Llandeilo (with
evidence specific to this line, such as captioned photographs, shed allocations at Llandeilo or
Llandovery, and published line histories), liveries and colours (to model them), typical train
length, the passenger and goods mix, and dated events (takeovers, closures, accidents,
centenaries). Say plainly what is specific to this line and what is only typical of the region.

## Places

Llandeilo station and the junction for Carmarthen; Ffairfach; Llandybïe; Derwydd Road; Tirydail
(Ammanford); Pantyffynnon; the Carmarthen branch stations (Llandilo Bridge, Golden Grove,
Dryslwyn, Llanarthney, Nantgaredig, Abergwili); the Vale of Towy stations north to Llandovery
(Talley Road, Glanrhyd, Llangadog, Llanwrda); Glanrhyd bridge; Llandovery shed.

## Method and source priority

Every claim from a source actually read, with what it says. Cross-check documented claims against
two independent sources and mark each cross-checked or single-source. Contradictions side by side,
never silently resolved. Wikipedia is a lead, not a citation on its own. Prefer Coflein, Cadw,
Heneb (Dyfed Archaeological Trust), the National Library of Wales, People's Collection Wales,
Network Rail and Transport for Wales, railway heritage bodies, published line histories, and
RailUK or Disused Stations as secondary. At most 45 WebSearch calls (a shared budget); prefer
WebFetch on known URLs.

## Output

Two files only, no commit: this brief, and the note
[`docs/research/railway-later.md`](../railway-later.md) with the standard frontmatter, sections per
period, a "What a modeller needs" section with confidence labels, open questions, and a numbered
Sources list in the format `tools/research/extract-sources.mjs` reads.
