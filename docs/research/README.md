documented, what is reconstructed by analogy, and what is unknown.

The first six were produced in one pass on 2026-09-26, with WebSearch quota exhausted part way
through (see [`process.md`](process.md)); `conversations-by-class` was added on 2026-09-28 and the rest
on 2026-10-02.

**Independent checks.** `conversations-by-class` had a verification pass on 2026-09-28 (recorded in
the note). On 2026-10-02 fourteen notes were checked independently against their sources, each
check kept in [`reviews/`](reviews/); the corrections are applied in the notes (each marked
*Corrected 2026-10-02 (independent check)*), except where the table says otherwise. *Corrected
2026-10-02*: this index used to list eight notes and say none had had an independent check; by then
that was wrong on both counts.

**Why checked notes are still `draft`.** A note moves to `reviewed` only once its check's corrections
are applied **and** the decisions its check leaves for the owner are settled. Six checks have an open
"For the owner's decision" section (iron age, medieval, victorian, deep time, ripples from afar,
invasions and raids; for example which trains to draw from 1873, or whether to show St Teilo's
tower from the 1400s), and the language check leaves one model decision for Dewi. The other checked
notes (timeline, event-effects, railway-later, railway-locomotives, anglo-norman-and-middle-english,
roman-arrival) have no owner decisions, but nobody has yet confirmed that their corrections were applied
in full, and the two railway checks list claims they did not check. Building-models' corrections are
not marked in the note. So none is moved to `reviewed` on 2026-10-02; these six are the first
candidates once a pass confirms the corrections.

## Citing these notes

Each note's `## Sources` list is extracted into `src/content/generated/sources.ts` by
`node tools/research/extract-sources.mjs`, and content cites sources as `src('medieval:S27')`.
An unknown id is a compile error, and CI fails if the generated file is out of date. If a note's
sources are renumbered, every citation of it must be re-mapped by title, not by number.
