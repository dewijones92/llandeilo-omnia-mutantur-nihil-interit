| Layer | Tool | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Types | `tsc` (strict) | Provenance cannot be documented without a source; a heard ambient sound point cannot have a level without a provenance; citations must exist; feature kinds are exhaustive |
| Unit | Vitest (`src/**/*.test.ts`) | Timeline mapping, year formatting incl. fractional slider years, heightfield sampling, feature presence windows, half-open era lookup, key-date stepping, snapping and camera shots; sun position (noon due south, east/west, short winter days), day phases, lighting by hour (warm dusk, moonlit night with stars and windows, mist, smooth changes), season looks and the climate snow line, `?hour=`/`?season=` parsing; ambient sound tracks: interpolation between points, fade-out to a silent point, silence before the first point, no fade-in from silence, and each sounding bed's reason (`since`) and the reason it is fading towards; the guess game's clue ranking (tightest bracket first, firm before probable on a tie, firm only with exact dates, imagined never, a fading phase never, only the new phase where two meet at the year), the climate clue's span and whether the season's snow shows it, the same-scene check (features, people, mapped woodland, climate), round picking and scoring; the lowest height snow can lie; when the Begin card shows (deep links, `?debug`, automated browsers and `?begin=` overrides); the graphics levels (choice order of `?quality=`, `?fx=low` and the remembered choice, each level shedding cost, tree density, the debug line) |
| Content integrity | Vitest (`tests/content.test.ts`) | Unique ids; every place, person and source reference resolves; documented items have sources; conversations are imagined; an era for every slider position; key dates far enough apart to scrub; an up-to-date voice clip per line and no orphans; a licence record for every shipped file; a camera shot for every event, inside the disc, naming only real features; a language note for every conversation in an old language; no speech before about AD 500 labelled Welsh; trains cover the railway's life, one at a time; every heard sound point has a level, resolvable sources and the words its tier asks for, in date order on the timeline; no bed is heard before the point that names it (every gap scanned); no bell or train before the railway of January 1857 (scanned to the day before); the guess game (`tests/guess.test.ts`) has five or more rounds outside deep time, and every clue's dates contain the year with strength matching provenance and exact dates, only documented features claim exact dates, and the Roman fort, Fan Camp and Dinefwr are probable clues; trains cover the railway's life, one at a time; |
